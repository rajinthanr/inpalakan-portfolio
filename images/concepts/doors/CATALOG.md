## 1. Strong Teak Multi-Panel & Slatted Designs (வலுவான பலகைப் பிரிப்புத் தேக்குக் கதவுகள்)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Built with intermediate divider rails & slats ("slacks in between")**: These designs avoid a weak single flat slab. Instead, they feature heavy seasoned teak perimeter stiles, robust horizontal divider rails, and vertical mullions framing individual raised panels or segmented slats. This ensures structural strength, durability against seasonal temperature/humidity expansion, and authentic artisan joinery.
