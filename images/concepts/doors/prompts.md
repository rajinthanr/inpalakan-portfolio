# Teak door concept references

These AI-generated images are design references, not photographs of completed Inpalakan Timber work. The set follows the remaining approved work in the project: strong teak construction, several smaller slatted sections, visible rails and stiles, restrained carving, and practical joinery. The previously removed concepts were treated as rejected directions.

## Shared generation direction

Photorealistic, front-facing premium Sri Lankan teak entrance door concept inspired by Inpalakan Timber's completed craftsmanship. Use genuine warm teak grain, thick rails and stiles, multiple smaller slatted or louver-like timber sections with visible gaps and shadow lines, realistic workshop-buildable joinery, restrained traditional carving, neutral architectural setting, soft daylight, and no text or watermark. Avoid a single uninterrupted slab, oversized story panels, excessive ornament, and weak-looking members.
