# Gypsum ceiling samples

These ten reference concepts use practical gypsum-board forms, clean finished surfaces, concealed LED coves, and balanced recessed lighting. Three generated directions were rejected and excluded: overlapping rounded panels, a complex layered lotus, and an uneven diamond layout.

1. **01-double-step-rectangle.png** — Rectangular double-step floating tray.
2. **02-octagon-circle-tray.png** — Octagonal tray with a circular centre recess.
3. **03-green-star-centre.png** — Four-point star with a restrained green accent.
4. **04-s-curve-hall.png** — Continuous S-curve lighting bands.
5. **05-concentric-ring-halo.png** — Concentric circular halo layers.
6. **06-connected-rectangle-islands.png** — Three connected rectangular floating islands.
7. **07-scalloped-corner-tray.png** — Rectangular tray with simple scalloped corners.
8. **08-rounded-rectangle-tray.png** — Two-level rounded-rectangle tray.
9. **09-simple-cross-feature.png** — Straight, joined cross-shaped centre feature.
10. **10-symmetrical-square-tray.png** — Centred square-within-square tray with equal spacing.
