# Metallic TV-unit samples

These ten concepts use metallic-painted slab-front cabinetry, concealed wiring, practical closed storage, and restrained illuminated display areas. Timber, stone, and fluted surfaces appear only as feature-wall accents.

1. **01-metallic-teal-display-wall.png** — Teal base storage and twin display towers.
2. **02-graphite-champagne-wall.png** — Graphite base with champagne towers and a dark stone centre.
3. **03-pearl-grey-corner-unit.png** — Compact pearl-grey corner media unit.
4. **04-midnight-blue-media-wall.png** — Midnight-blue symmetrical display wall.
5. **05-champagne-charcoal-media-wall.png** — Champagne cabinetry around a charcoal TV panel.
6. **06-graphite-asymmetric-unit.png** — Graphite console with one tall tower and floating shelves.
7. **07-sage-rounded-towers.png** — Metallic sage unit with rounded display ends.
8. **08-bronze-gunmetal-wall.png** — Bronze towers with gunmetal base storage.
9. **09-layered-rectangle-wall.png** — Creative rectangles-within-rectangles modular composition.
10. **10-media-wall-desk.png** — Metallic green media wall with an integrated work desk.

## Online design references

The creative directions were informed by Houzz guidance on dividing built-ins into base and upper elements, concealing equipment and cables, combining open display with closed storage, and integrating media walls with other functions:

## Sri Lankan open-hall concepts

11. **11-graphite-bronze-sri-lankan-hall.png** — A broad graphite-and-bronze wall unit in a tiled Sri Lankan living hall with grilled timber windows and an adjoining dining room.
12. **12-open-plan-divider-unit.png** — A floor-supported champagne-and-charcoal media divider for an open living and dining room.
13. **13-under-stair-sage-unit.png** — Metallic sage fitted cabinetry following the staircase line in a practical family hall.
14. **14-pearl-grey-open-hall.png** — A wide pearl-grey media wall with simple rounded display ends and copper accents.
15. **15-column-integrated-blue-unit.png** — A midnight-blue media unit fitted between structural columns with a bench extension and open dining area beyond.

These scenes intentionally use familiar Sri Lankan residential details: polished tiled floors, ceiling fans, timber doors and grilled windows, tropical daylight, moderate ceiling heights, and connected living/dining layouts.
