## Technical Specifications & Inpalakan Timbers Standards

- **Primary Timber Options**:
  - **Ceylon Teak (தேக்கு)**: Naturally water-resistant, rich golden-honey grain, natural oils resist termites and powder-post beetles common in tropical Sri Lanka.
  - **Mahogany (மஹோகனி)**: Dense, reddish-brown hardwood with smooth grain; ideal for deep satin finishes and classical almirahs.
  - **Nadun (நதுன்)**: Dense local hardwood known for high structural weight capacity and deep natural figuring.
  - **Moisture-Resistant Marine Plywood (MR / BWR)**: High-density waterproof ply used for internal carcasses, faced with matching natural timber veneer or anti-scratch laminate.
- **Joinery Standards**: Mortise-and-tenon door frames, solid hardwood drawer boxes with dovetail or rebated joinery, and heavy-duty 45kg load-rated soft-close drawer runners.
- **Hardware**: Concealed 35mm soft-close hinges (Hafele / Blum / DTC), heavy-duty aluminium sliding systems with bottom-weighted anti-jump rollers, and solid brass or brushed aluminium architectural pull handles.
- **Pest & Moisture Protection**: Every cupboard is elevated on solid hardwood sub-plinths with water-resistant sealants to protect against damp floors during mopping and wet weather.
