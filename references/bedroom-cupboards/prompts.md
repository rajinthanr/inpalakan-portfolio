# Bedroom cupboard design samples for Sri Lankan homes

These 10 reference concepts showcase bedroom cupboards and wardrobes designed specifically for Sri Lankan residential environments, balancing tropical airflow, high ceiling heights, solid teak timber joinery, saree/traditional clothing storage, and modern space-saving configurations.

1. **01-sl-teak-cane-rattan-wardrobe.jpg** — Tropical modernism 4-door wardrobe crafted in solid Ceylon teak wood with natural woven cane rattan webbing door panel inserts for natural airflow in tropical humidity, vertical brass handles, king bed with white linen, and casement window overlooking palm trees.
2. **02-sl-floor-to-ceiling-loft-wardrobe.jpg** — Full-height 9-foot floor-to-ceiling wardrobe with separate top loft cabinets (மட்டு மட்டம்) for storing extra bedding and travel suitcases, 4 lower hinged teak doors with long vertical timber handles, and exposed ceiling rafters.
3. **03-sl-sliding-mirror-vanity-wardrobe.jpg** — 3-track sliding door wardrobe with one full-length silver mirror door and warm teak wood grain, adjoined seamlessly with a matching built-in dressing table, back-lit mirror, and soft-close vanity drawers.
4. **04-sl-fitted-bed-suite-overhead-bridge.jpg** — Fitted master bedroom suite with twin floor-to-ceiling wardrobes flanking the bed, connected by overhead hydraulic bridge cabinets, recessed LED reading downlights, bedside floating niches, and under-bed storage drawers.
5. **05-sl-classical-carved-teak-almirah.jpg** — Classical Sri Lankan family heritage 3-door almirah in solid seasoned teak wood, featuring a gentle arched crown moulding, hand-carved floral rosette accents, center beveled oval dressing mirror, and traditional brass key locks.
6. **06-sl-two-tone-minimalist-corner-shelves.jpg** — Contemporary urban wardrobe in matte off-white finish framed with warm solid teak accents, recessed J-pull handles, and an integrated curved 5-tier open timber corner display shelf for bags and perfumes.
7. **07-sl-internal-saree-wardrobe-organization.jpg** — Open-door internal organization showcase featuring custom pull-out multi-tier padded saree racks, verti/sarong hanging bar, dual shirt rails, locked velvet-lined jewelry drawers, and sensor LED strip lighting.
8. **08-sl-corner-l-shaped-fitted-wardrobe.jpg** — Space-optimizing L-shaped corner wardrobe with 135-degree bi-fold corner doors eliminating dead corner space, full-length dress hanging rail, and upper storage shelves in natural honey teak.
9. **09-sl-study-desk-bookshelf-wardrobe.jpg** — Multi-functional student/children's bedroom wardrobe integrating a 2-door clothes closet, open bookshelf tiers, pull-out study desk, and overhead closed stationery cupboards.
10. **10-sl-fluted-timber-smoked-glass-wardrobe.jpg** — Luxury modern bedroom wardrobe with vertical solid teak fluted side panels, slim black aluminum-framed smoked bronze glass doors, and warm 3000K vertical LED interior illumination.

## Modern Metallic Paint Finish Bedroom Wardrobes (மெட்டாலிக் நவீன படுக்கையறை அலமாரிகள்)

11. **11-sl-champagne-graphite-metallic-wardrobe-mirror.jpg** — High-end modern master bedroom fitted wardrobe with 2K automotive metallic paint finish (champagne metallic upper doors and dark graphite metallic lower drawers), flush-integrated 4ft × 14in vertical grooming mirror with beveled edges and warm LED halo backlighting, seamless J-pull handle profiles, and large window with tropical palms.
12. **12-sl-rosegold-charcoal-metallic-granite-vanity.jpg** — Luxury bedroom wardrobe suite featuring satin rose-gold metallic doors and charcoal towers, adjoined to an integrated dressing vanity with a solid 40mm Jet Black granite countertop and vertical 4ft × 14in dressing mirror.
13. **13-sl-bronze-pearl-metallic-sliding-mirror.jpg** — 3-door sliding wardrobe in rich bronze metallic and pearl-white automotive lacquer, full-height silver mirror door panel, and Tan Brown granite accessory dressing shelf.
14. **14-sl-sage-cream-metallic-desk-mirror.jpg** — Contemporary metallic sage green and cream wardrobe unit with integrated study/dressing desk, granite writing insert, and vertical 4ft × 14in grooming mirror.
15. **15-sl-midnight-blue-champagne-granite-suite.jpg** — Luxury master bedroom walk-in suite with midnight blue metallic cabinetry, champagne trim, central accessory island with polished Black Galaxy granite countertop, and vertical mirror.
