# Custom furniture concept references

These nine AI-generated images are client design ideas. They are not photos of completed Inpalakan Timbers work. The PNG files are the original generated images; the WebP files are the smaller copies used by the site.

## Shared image direction

Generate one photorealistic image per design in a believable, modest Sri Lankan home. Show the whole furniture piece from a clear eye-level three-quarter view, with tiled floors and natural daylight. Use realistic supports, joints, cabinet depths, handles, and drawer clearances. Avoid people, text, logos, watermarks, and lavish showroom styling.

2. **FUR-502 — Storage bed with side tables:** Timber platform bed with a slatted headboard, two usable under-bed drawers, and matching one-drawer side tables.
3. **FUR-503 — Study desk with bookshelf:** Compact timber desk with adult knee space, a three-drawer pedestal, open shelves, cable access, and a matching chair.
4. **FUR-504 — Living room display sideboard:** Freestanding cabinet with two closed doors, three drawers, an open shelf, and a framed glass display bay with soft light.

6. **FUR-506 — Timber sofa set:** Three-seat timber sofa and two matching armchairs with removable sage cushions and visible seat support.
8. **FUR-508 — Open room divider bookshelf:** Stable open shelving between living and dining spaces, with two low closed cupboards and clear walking space.
9. **FUR-509 — Coffee table with drawers:** Supported rectangular table with two useful drawers and a lower shelf, with one drawer partly open.
10. **FUR-510 — Dressing table with mirror:** Timber table with two drawers, a side cupboard, a matching stool and a portrait 4 ft × 18 in mirror; no granite.
