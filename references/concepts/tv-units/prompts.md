# Metallic-finish TV unit concepts

These five AI-generated images are client design ideas, not photos of completed Inpalakan Timbers work. The PNG files are the original generated images. Smaller WebP copies are used in the site gallery.

## Shared generation direction

Create one photorealistic TV-unit concept per image in a believable Sri Lankan living room, with tiled floors, natural daylight and realistic room scale. Use the smooth, paint-ready cupboard style of the bedroom concepts: satin metallic paint, sealed edges, neat joints, a supported plinth, practical handles, suitable drawer and door hardware, cable access and device ventilation. Use shutter board only where the fabricator confirms it is suitable for the structure. Mount the TV to a suitable structural wall or support. Show the whole unit from an eye-level three-quarter view. Avoid timber grain, cane, excessive gold, unsupported parts, people, text, logos and watermarks.
