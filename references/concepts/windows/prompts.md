# Teak window concepts for Jaffna homes

These five AI-generated images are client design ideas, not photos of completed Inpalakan Timbers work. The PNG files are the original generated images. Smaller WebP copies are used in the gallery. The concepts take their two- and three-shutter proportions, textured glass, cross rails, and metal grilles from the completed window photos in `public/images/projects/windows/`.

## Shared generation direction

Create one realistic teak window per image in a modest Jaffna home with plaster walls, tiled floors, and natural daylight. Use rectangular frames, repeatable straight rails and stiles, neat joints, practical hinges and reachable locks. Show a separate close-spaced steel grille fixed into the masonry reveal, and leave clearance for the shutters to open. Include a weather-shedding sill. Show the full window from an eye-level three-quarter view. Avoid oversized glazing, unusual window shapes, complex carved parts, people, text, logos, and watermarks.

The images are visual ideas only. The workshop must confirm timber sections, fixings, locks, glazing, opening direction, and any required emergency exit before making a window. No concept is presented as certified or guaranteed burglar-proof.

## Concepts

1. **WIN-501 — Three-shutter teak window with top glass:** Textured glass in three shutters, fixed glass panes above them, and straight steel bars visible behind an open shutter.
2. **WIN-502 — Two-shutter teak window with solid lower panels:** Textured upper glass, solid teak lower panels, and an independent steel grille.
3. **WIN-503 — Diagonal teak glazing bars:** Two simple shutters with diagonal glazing bars, textured glass, and straight grille bars.
4. **WIN-504 — Compact teak kitchen window:** Two shutters with horizontal rails and textured glass above a kitchen sink, with a grille behind.
5. **WIN-505 — Three-shutter teak window with solid panels:** Textured upper glass and solid lower panels in three shutters, with the centre shutter partly open.
