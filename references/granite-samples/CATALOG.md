## Technical Specifications & Inpalakan Timbers Standards

- **Stone Types**: Jet Black / Absolute Black Granite, Black Galaxy Granite, Tan Brown Granite.
- **Slab Thickness**: 20mm single slab and 40mm laminated front edge profiles.
- **Timber Integration**: Installed directly over Inpalakan Timbers custom-crafted solid Ceylon teak and marine carcass frames with reinforced structural cleats.
- **Protection**: Factory diamond-buffed and sealed with food-safe penetrating hydrophobic/oleophobic sealers resisting turmeric, oil, and citrus.
