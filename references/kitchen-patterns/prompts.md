# Metallic kitchen cupboard samples

These ten sample concepts use clean, flat single-board slab doors and drawers with straight square edges. The finishes are metallic painted surfaces with a restrained satin shimmer. Bevels, framed or shaker profiles, raised panels, routed borders, arches, mouldings, and carvings are intentionally excluded.

1. **01-champagne-metallic-l-shape.png** — Champagne metallic L-shaped kitchen with handle channels.
2. **02-graphite-metallic-u-shape.png** — Graphite metallic U-shaped kitchen with slim black pulls.
3. **03-sage-metallic-galley.png** — Sage metallic galley kitchen with recessed black channels.
4. **04-pearl-silver-metallic-l-shape.png** — Pearl silver metallic L-shaped kitchen with minimal steel pulls.
5. **05-bronze-metallic-peninsula.png** — Bronze metallic kitchen with a compact breakfast peninsula.
6. **06-midnight-blue-metallic.png** — Midnight blue metallic kitchen with brushed-steel pulls.
7. **07-rose-gold-charcoal-metallic.png** — Rose-gold overhead cupboards with charcoal base cupboards.
8. **08-pearl-white-metallic-galley.png** — Pearl white handleless galley kitchen.
9. **09-teal-metallic-corner.png** — Metallic teal corner kitchen with a timber display niche.
10. **10-gunmetal-champagne-island.png** — Gunmetal main cabinetry with a champagne metallic island.
11. **11-sl-champagne-graphite-granite-mirror.jpg** — Two-tone champagne & graphite metallic painted kitchen with Jet Black granite countertop, undermount sink, and integrated 4ft x 14in vertical grooming mirror.
12. **12-sl-bronze-pearl-tan-brown-granite-mirror.jpg** — Bronze & pearl white metallic L-shaped kitchen with polished Tan Brown granite worktop, built-in cooktop, and 4ft x 14in mirror on pantry end panel.
13. **13-sl-midnight-blue-black-granite-island-mirror.jpg** — Midnight blue metallic painted base cabinets, black granite waterfall breakfast island with carved drainboard flutes, and 4ft x 14in LED halo mirror.
14. **14-sl-sage-green-black-galaxy-granite-mirror.jpg** — Metallic sage green & cream U-shaped kitchen with Black Galaxy granite countertop, double undermount sink, and 4ft x 14in dressing mirror.
